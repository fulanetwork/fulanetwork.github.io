How the bridge works
  • This is the official lock & release bridge for FULA. Tokens are held by the bridge contract on one chain and released on the other — nothing is minted or burned, so total supply never changes.
  • You pay one small fee in ETH on the source chain. Delivery on the destination is automatic — you don't need ETH there.
  • Amounts are precise to 6 decimals (0.000001 FULA). Anything smaller stays in your wallet.
  • If a transfer is delayed, it is queued — not lost. It can always be completed later, and this page will show you a Retry option when that applies.
  • Bridging on Ethereum needs 2 wallet confirmations (Approve, then Bridge). Repeat transfers may need only 1.
⚠️ Important Information

FULA Token is a utility token intended solely for use within the Fula network ecosystem. It does not represent an investment, security, or share in any company, and it does not provide any ownership, voting rights, or entitlement to profits.

The bridge moves your own FULA tokens between the Ethereum and Base networks; it does not buy, sell, or exchange tokens. Cross-chain transfers depend on third-party infrastructure (LayerZero) and public blockchains, and delivery times can vary. The value of FULA may fluctuate significantly and may become worthless. Participation is entirely at your own risk.
